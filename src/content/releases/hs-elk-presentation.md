The honest-engineering beat stayed in: the deadline story is told with the board's own
numbers, because the audience that matters can tell when a failure slide has been sanded
down.

The occasion is on the public record: the festival's
[Winners Showcase listing](https://forum.mutek.org/en/shows/2026/music-hackspace-x-mutek-hackathon-winners-showcase)
names HRNSXTN x RDMSXN for the Elk Audio challenge, among five winning projects from a
weekend where 66 builders shipped 26 working prototypes.
