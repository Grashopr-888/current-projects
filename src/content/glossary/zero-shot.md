---
term: 'Zero shot'
area: methods
definition: >-
  Using a pretrained model on a task with no task specific training. Windchime's audio
  language models retrieve stems zero shot: text and audio already share one embedding space,
  so retrieval works from the first stem, and finetuning on the corpus becomes an option as
  it grows.
related: [contrastive-learning, clap, alm, retrieval]
---
