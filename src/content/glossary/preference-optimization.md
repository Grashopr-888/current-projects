---
term: 'Preference optimization (RLHF, DPO)'
area: methods
definition: >-
  Tuning a model toward outputs people prefer, learned from ranked pairs rather than one
  correct answer: reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), or direct preference
  optimization (DPO). A future direction here: HRNSXTN's planned blind A/B listening test and
  the scenes a Lichtspiel performer keeps gather exactly this kind of paired judgment.
related: [post-training, supervised-finetuning]
