---
term: Granular synthesis
product: hrnsxtn
definition: >-
  Building sound from very short slices ("grains") of recorded audio, windowed and
  overlapped. HRNSXTN's latent variant chooses which grains to play by matching in a learned
  embedding space, so the output is always real recorded material — intelligently selected,
  never synthesized from scratch.
related: ['Knowledge distillation', 'Embedding space']
---
