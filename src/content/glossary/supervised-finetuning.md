---
term: 'Supervised finetuning (SFT)'
area: methods
definition: >-
  Finetuning on labelled pairs of input and desired output, the usual first post training
  step for language models. Windchime logs every good generation as a prompt and plan pair, a
  dataset for tuning its planner as the log grows.
related: [finetuning, preference-optimization, in-context-learning, synthetic-data]
