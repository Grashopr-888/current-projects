---
term: 'Quantization'
area: methods
definition: >-
  Storing weights or vectors in fewer bits, such as 8 bit integers in place of 32 bit floats,
  to save memory and time. Quantizing music2latent would have bought 2 to 3 times where
  HRNSXTN needed 30 to 100; its matching table went to 8 bit instead, with the ordering
  unchanged.
related: [int8-table, pruning, knowledge-distillation, post-training]
---
