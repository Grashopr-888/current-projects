---
term: CLAP
product: windchime
definition: >-
  Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining. A family of audio language models trained with
  contrastive learning to map text and audio into one shared space, so a spoken phrase can
  retrieve matching sound. Windchime uses CLAP models zero shot as feature extractors; tuning
  one on the corpus is a future direction as it grows.
related: [alm, embedding-space, contrastive-learning, zero-shot]
